> 01. 0:NADIR

0:Nadir is a space shooter where every run makes the next one stronger. Blast through escalating enemy waves, bank upgrade currency, and spend it on a deep incremental upgrade tree — then pick your poison across multiple game modes, from endless survival runs to focused, high-pressure challenges.

> 02. NIHONGO

GENRE:
Learning · Japanese
PLATFORM:
ANDROID
STATUS:
In early development

NihonGO is a Japanese learning app built like a game. Drill hiragana, katakana, and kanji in bite-sized lessons, lock words in with spaced-repetition reviews, and build real vocabulary — with streaks, levels, and progress tracking to keep you showing up every day.

> 03. MCS

GENRE:
Idle clicker · Parody
PLATFORM:
ANDROID
STATUS:
In early development

MCS (Main Character Syndrome) is an idle clicker that parodies social media. You're the one real person on a map full of self-aware NPCs: tap, post, and watch your hearts and followers climb as a whole fake internet opens up around you — parody networks, a bank that loses your money on purpose, and a small hidden game inside nearly every app.

> 04. BLOCK CRUSHER

GENRE:
Puzzle · Arcade
PLATFORM:
ANDROID
STATUS:
Coming to Google Play

Block Crusher is a block-smashing arcade puzzler for Android. Clear the board, push for a new best score, and spend the coins you earn on power-ups and cosmetics — no accounts, nothing to buy, and your progress stays on your phone.

> 05. SUNKEEPER

GENRE:
Idle incremental · Galaxy
PLATFORM:
STEAM

Tend a newborn star in the corner of your desktop. Gather light, raise worlds and guide their people from the first fire to the stars. Collapse your sun in a supernova, decipher a lost alien alphabet, and, at the very end, tear open a black hole into the multiverse.
