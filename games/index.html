Idle clicker · Parody

MCS

Android80+ NPCsHidden mini-games

MCS (Main Character Syndrome) is an idle clicker that parodies social media. You're the one real person on a map full of self-aware NPCs: tap, post, and watch your hearts and followers climb as a whole fake internet opens up around you — parody networks, a bank that loses your money on purpose, and a small hidden game inside nearly every app.
