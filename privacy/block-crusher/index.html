Block Crusher Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Applies to the Block Crusher game for Android (package com.blockcrusher)

Block Crusher has no accounts and asks you for no personal information. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your device goes to Google, to show ads and to ask for your consent to them where the law requires it.

Who we are

Block Crusher is made by Beniah Gepts, an independent developer based in Belgium (beniah.games). For anything about this policy or your data, write to contact@beniah.games.

What stays on your device

The game keeps the following on your phone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • your progress, scores and best results, including saved runs;
  • coins, power-ups and the cosmetics you have unlocked or equipped;
  • your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and controls.

This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If Android's own backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy it to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone; that copy is held by Google under your account, and we cannot see it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with the ads you see.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices and on where you live. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Your consent to ads (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. No ads are loaded until you have answered. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Options › Ad privacy choices.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google's advertising services described above.

Purchases

The game does not currently take any payment. If real in-app purchases are added in a later version, they will be processed by Google Play, we will never see your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Block Crusher is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you ourselves. You can withdraw or change your consent to ads in the game at any time, as described above. For the advertising data Google processes, you can use the controls in your Google account and phone settings, or contact Google. If you live in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Belgium that is the Data Protection Authority.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the game.
